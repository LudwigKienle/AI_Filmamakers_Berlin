Runway AIF Community Screening

16 July 2026 · IL KINO, Berlin-Neukölln

AI Filmmakers Berlin hosted Runway’s first community AIF screening. Together with Runway, we brought the winning films of this year’s Runway AI Film Festival to the big screen at IL KINO in Berlin-Neukölln.

It was a full house: drinks and community time from 20:00, then the screening at 21:00, with free entry for everyone who RSVPed.

  • Winning films of the Runway AI Film Festival 2026 on the big screen
  • Runway’s first community AIF screening, hosted in Berlin
  • Drinks, conversations and community time before and after

Thank you

Thank you to Runway for the collaboration, to IL KINO for hosting us, and to everyone who came out on a summer night to watch AI films together.
