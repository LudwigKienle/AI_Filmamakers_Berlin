Spring Session 2026

28 May 2026 · Potsdamer Platz, Berlin

Around 100 filmmakers, artists, technologists and creative industry voices joined the AI Filmmakers Berlin Spring Session for an evening of screenings, conversations and exchange at Potsdamer Platz.

The programme brought together distinctly different works and creative approaches. The evening opened with Ben Chimoy’s When Is It Yours?, continuing the community’s conversation around authorship and ownership in AI filmmaking.

Films, voices and workflows

This edition featured work by Shamila Lengsfeld, Seb Ungrad, Cadmo Quintero, Loïs Levy, Farzin Raisstousi, Phil Langer and Mark Wachholz.

Julien Raout shared an update from the World AI Film Festival, followed by Seb Ungrad’s practical Magnific workflow session.

  • Selected AI film screenings and filmmaker conversations
  • World AI Film Festival presentation and creative workflow session
  • Live audience voting for the first AI Filmmakers Berlin Audience Award
  • Conversations and community exchange throughout the evening

Our first Audience Award

The audience voted live during the event and selected Mark Wachholz’s The Interview as the winner. The physical award was designed by Ben Chimoy and Elham Maddah — the beginning of a tradition we hope to continue.
